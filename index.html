In short: Tikki works on your phone. It has no account and no servers, and it does not collect, sell or share your personal data. What you enter stays on your device unless you choose to export or share it.

1. What Tikki stores

To work, Tikki saves the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Your routines and events: names, times, repeat rules, categories, notes, locations, people's names and meeting links you type in.
  • Your daily records: what you marked done or skipped, goal progress, focus-timer minutes, checklists, mood and day notes.
  • Your profile: the name you enter and, if you choose one, a profile photo (copied into the app's private folder).
  • Your settings, such as reminders, theme and language.
  • If you turn on App lock: your PIN, stored only as a salted, one-way hash in Android's encrypted storage. The PIN itself is never saved.

The developer has no access to this information. Tikki does not upload it to the developer or to any third-party server.

2. What we collect

We do not collect any personal data through Tikki. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it has no user accounts. Tikki also does not request Android's internet permission, so the app itself cannot send your data over the internet.

4. Voice input

If you use the optional voice Quick add, your speech is converted to text by your phone's speech recognition service (on most Android phones this is provided by Google). That service may process audio under its own privacy policy. Tikki receives only the resulting text, uses it to fill in the new activity, and does not store or send the audio.

5. Sharing your data

Tikki does not share your data with anyone. Data leaves your device only when you choose to do so, for example:

  • creating a backup file and sending or saving it with the app you pick (such as Google Drive or a messaging app);
  • sharing an activity, a monthly report image or a PDF through Android's share menu;
  • opening a meeting link or a map location, which opens in another app.

Once shared, that copy is handled by the app or service you chose, under its own privacy policy.

6. Google Play

Tikki is distributed through Google Play. Google handles downloads, updates and ratings under its own privacy policy. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard visitor logs under GitHub's own policy. The site itself has no analytics or cookies.

  • Ratings and reviews: Tikki may show Google Play's review popup. Google handles this popup and anything you submit. Tikki never sees your rating, your review or whether you left one. Reviews you post publicly on Google Play can be read by the developer and others, as on any app.
  • App updates: Tikki asks Google Play whether a newer version is available and, if you choose to update, Google Play downloads and installs it. Tikki sends none of your routines, notes or other content for this.
  • Statistics: Google Play gives developers statistics about their apps, such as install numbers, and technical crash and performance reports. Tikki does not add your routines, notes or other content to these.

7. Keeping and deleting data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can erase everything at any time in Settings → Erase all data, or by uninstalling Tikki, which removes its stored data from your phone. Backup files you exported are kept wherever you saved them until you delete them.

8. Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. You can add App lock with a PIN or fingerprint/face and hide reminder text on the lock screen. Please keep exported backup files somewhere safe, as anyone with the file can read its contents.

9. Your choices

Because your data is on your own device, you control it directly. You can view and edit it in the app, export it to a backup file, erase it, and turn off notifications, alarms or the microphone in your phone's settings. We hold no copy to access, correct or delete on your behalf.

10. Children

Tikki is a general productivity app and is not directed at children under 13. Because Tikki does not collect personal data, no personal data is collected from children.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new effective date. If a change ever involved collecting data, it would be explained in the app before taking effect.
