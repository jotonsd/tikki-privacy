Tikki Privacy Policy

App: Tikki – Daily Routine & Habits (Android, package com.jotonsutradhar.tikki)
Developer: Joton Sutradhar · Effective date: 4 November 2026

In short: Tikki works entirely on your phone. It has no account and no servers, and it does not collect, sell or share your personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device unless you choose to export it.

1. Information Tikki stores

To work, Tikki saves the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Your routines and events: names, times, repeat rules, categories, notes, locations, people's names and meeting links you type in.
  • Your daily records: what you marked done or skipped, goal progress, focus-timer minutes, checklists, mood and day notes.
  • Your profile: the name you enter and, if you choose one, a profile photo (copied into the app's private folder).
  • Your settings, such as reminders, theme and language.
  • If you turn on App lock: your PIN, stored only as a salted, one-way hash in Android's encrypted storage. The PIN itself is never saved.

The developer has no access to any of this information. It is never uploaded to the developer or to any third-party server.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal or device data. Tikki contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it has no user accounts.

3. Permissions and why they are used

PermissionWhy
NotificationsTo show reminders before and when your activities start, missed-activity checks and summaries.
Alarms & reminders (exact alarms)So reminders arrive on time, even in battery-saving mode.
Full-screen alarmOnly for items you set to "Ring as alarm", so the alarm can wake the screen and show Stop, Snooze and Done.

4. Voice input

If you use the optional voice Quick add, your speech is converted to text by your phone's speech recognition service (for most Android phones this is provided by Google). That service may process audio under its own privacy policy. Tikki receives only the resulting text, uses it to fill in the new activity, and does not store or send the audio.

5. Sharing your data

Tikki does not share your data with anyone. Data leaves your device only when you choose to do so, for example:

  • creating a backup file and sending or saving it with the app you pick (such as Google Drive or a messaging app);
  • sharing an activity, a monthly report image or a PDF through Android's share menu;
  • opening a meeting link or a map location, which opens in another app;
  • leaving a rating or review in the Google Play popup that Tikki may show. Google handles this popup and anything you submit under its own policies; Tikki never sees your rating, your review or whether you left one.

Once shared, that copy is handled by the app or service you chose, under its own privacy policy.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can erase everything at any time in Settings → Erase all data, or by uninstalling Tikki, which removes all of its stored data from your phone. Backup files you exported are kept wherever you saved them until you delete them.

7. Security

Data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. You can add App lock with a PIN or fingerprint/face and hide reminder text on the lock screen. Please keep exported backup files somewhere safe, as anyone with the file can read its contents.

8. Children

Tikki is a general productivity app and is not directed at children under 13. Because Tikki does not collect personal data, no personal data is collected from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new effective date. If a change ever involved collecting data, it would be explained in the app before taking effect.
