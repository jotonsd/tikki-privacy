FAQ

Good to know

Is Tikki free?

Yes, Tikki is free to download and shows no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up. Open the app and start planning.

Where is my data kept?

On your phone, in the app's private storage. The developer cannot see it.

What if I change phones?

Create a backup file in Settings, move it to your new phone and restore it there.

Which phones does it work on?

Android phones running Android 8.0 or later.
