Tikki

Daily Routine & Habits, for Android

Tikki helps you plan a repeating day, tick it off and see how consistent you are. It works on your phone without an account, and it has no ads or analytics.

What Tikki does

  • Plan routines and one-time events, with flexible repeats, goals and checklists.
  • Get reminders on time, or set an item to ring as an alarm with Stop, Snooze and Done.
  • Mark items done or skipped, and use a focus timer.
  • Follow your streaks, charts, badges and monthly reports.
  • Use it in English or Bangla, in light or dark mode, with a home screen widget.
